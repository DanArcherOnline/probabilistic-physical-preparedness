# Probabilistic Physical Preparedness

**A framework for training toward the unknown.**

Most fitness paradigms prepare you for a *known* demand — a sport, a role, a specific peak. Probabilistic Physical Preparedness (PPP) starts from a different premise: the next physical challenge you face is unscripted, and the only meaningful goal is maximizing your statistical odds of succeeding at *any* of them.

This is a working notebook exploring that idea — the paradigm behind it, how it compares to conventional training models, and what it implies for how we should actually train.
